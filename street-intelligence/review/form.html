Pedestrian Map Review

Thank you for reviewing this map of the footpath on Old Nagardas Road, Andheri East. It was built from 34 phone walks along 220 m. The review takes about 30 minutes: open the map, try six short tasks, then answer a few questions. Please say out loud, or write down, anything that confuses you. There are no wrong answers. The map is being tested, not you.
