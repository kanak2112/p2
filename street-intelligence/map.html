Street Intelligence Map

A pedestrian map for one 220 m footpath in Andheri East, built on 15 recorded walks over 8 survey days. Every number is computed from those walks as you use it.

Screens

Try

  • Pinch, scroll or use + and − on the map. Zoomed in, the footpath becomes a ribbon as wide as the estimated room to walk.
  • Drag along the coloured strip or the room-to-walk ribbon. The spot, photos, overlays and counts follow your finger.
  • Change the date range or light filter on a footpath. Counts, colours and photos recompute.
  • Start a walk from Directions to see the walking space narrow and widen ahead of you.

Data rules

  • Blocked: the worst photo in a 10 m stretch scores an obstruction index of 1.4 or more.
  • Room to walk: proxemic band of that photo. Under 0.8 m intimate, 0.8 to 1.2 m social, 1.2 m or more clear. Estimated from photos, not tape-measured.
  • Dates are upload dates. The pilot videos lost their recording dates.
  • Crowding uses daylight photos only. Night segmentation is unreliable.
