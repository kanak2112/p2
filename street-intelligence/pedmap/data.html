Method · clear width is the free walkway traced in each 10 m from walk photos, as camera estimates, not tape measures; a stretch’s width is the median of the daytime walks that could measure it · blocked means the worst photo in the stretch scored obstruction 1.4 or higher · night light compares the walking surface in night photos with the route’s usual night frame; phones adjust exposure, so it ranks stretches and is not lux · one side of the road was walked · the underpass walkway (160–190 m) cannot be measured from photos.
